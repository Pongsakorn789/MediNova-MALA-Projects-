# Role Statement: Phorn Potjanasuj (6631503029), QA / Test Lead

**Team:** MediNova · **Project:** MALA Risk Screening & Monitoring System (MRSS) · **Live URL:** https://mala-project-2026.web.app

## What I owned

I owned the test plan, UAT structure, coded-session reporting, and SUS/TAM calculation record. The test strategy covers unit, integration, end-to-end, and user acceptance levels in [Test Plan](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/blob/main/Test_Plan_MediNova.md). The observed outcomes, participant limitations, and usability issues are in [UAT Results](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/blob/main/UAT_Results_MediNova.md); anonymised questionnaire data and calculations are in [sus_tam_responses.csv](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/blob/main/sus_tam_responses.csv).
I documented the comparative assessment results in the [comparison-table](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/tree/main/.docs/final/comparison-table), highlighting evaluation outcomes, differences, and areas requiring improvement based on the available evidence. Pending or unverified results are not reported as completed.

## Evidence

- [Test Plan](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/blob/main/Test_Plan_MediNova.md): defines the test levels, scenarios, success rule, measurement method, exit criteria, and severity definitions that I used to structure QA work.
- [UAT Results](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/blob/main/UAT_Results_MediNova.md): records the observer-form outcomes for U01–U09, task success rates, times, errors, participant conditions, usability observations, and limitations.
- [SUS/TAM responses](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/blob/main/sus_tam_responses.csv) and [SUS/TAM results](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/blob/main/sus_tam_results.md): provide the anonymised questionnaire input and the calculation record for the reported SUS and TAM values.
- [comparison-table](https://github.com/Pongsakorn789/MediNova-MALA-Projects-/tree/main/.docs/final/comparison-table): summarizes comparative assessment results in a structured table, making it easier to review differences, identify areas for improvement, and understand the overall evaluation outcomes.
